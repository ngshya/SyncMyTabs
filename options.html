Profiles
Open tabs sync
Mirrors your open tabs across devices on the same profile, using bookmarks as the transport. This is also the master switch — turning it off pauses tab groups and auto-archive too.
Paused — this device won't send or receive tab updates, and the tab-group and auto-archive checks are paused too.
days
Tab groups
Keeps a titled browser tab group's declared pages present, and keeps links clicked inside it from wandering outside the group. Chrome/Brave only.
⚠️ Turn off your browser's own tab-group sync (e.g. Chrome's account sync for tab groups, under its sync settings) for profiles managed here — having both try to sync group membership at once can conflict with this module's own reconcile checks.

Auto-archive idle tabs
Tracks the last time each of your tabs actually had focus. A tab you haven't looked at in a while is saved as a bookmark under SyncMyTabs/<profile>/_archive/<year>/<month>/<day>/ and then closed — pinned tabs and tabs in a browser tab group are never touched. Off by default.
